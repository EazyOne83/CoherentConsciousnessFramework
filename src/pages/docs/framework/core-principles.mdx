## Core Principles

The Coherent Consciousness Framework (CCF) is built on a set of foundational constructs
that define its modal architecture, information geometry, and dynamical behavior. These
constructs serve as the irreducible primitives of the theory — the elements from which all
higher-order dynamics, transitions, and emergent conscious states arise.

---

## 1. Modal Architecture

### **Physical Mode of Consciousness (PMC)**
PMC represents stability, embodiment, and constraint. It anchors the system in sensory
coherence, somatic grounding, and environmental coupling. PMC governs the system’s
relationship to physical reality and provides the stabilizing force that prevents runaway
dissolution.

### **Entropic Mode of Consciousness (EMC)**
EMC represents dissolution, uncertainty, and destabilization. It drives exploratory
processes, emotional turbulence, trauma states, and nonlinear transitions. EMC breaks
existing coherence structures, enabling reorganization and transformation.

### **Integrative Mode of Consciousness (IMC)**
IMC represents synthesis, meaning-making, and deep coherence. It governs insight,
reconstruction, and the emergence of stable, high-order conscious states. IMC is the
modal attractor associated with transformation, clarity, and wisdom.

---

## 2. Coherent Mode Consciousness State (CCS)

A **Coherent Mode Consciousness State (CCS)** emerges when PMC, EMC, and IMC achieve a
specific relational configuration that allows stable, integrative conscious experience.
CCS is not a mode — it is a **state** arising from modal interaction. CCS marks the
transition from fragmentation to coherence.

---

## 3. Integrative Information Flow (IIF)

The **Integrative Information Flow (IIF)** is the information-geometric manifold through
which consciousness moves. Its topology reflects:

- developmental history  
- connectivity structure  
- environmental coupling  
- learned patterns  
- trauma-induced distortions  

The IIF functions as a potential landscape, with each mode corresponding to an attractor
basin. The geometry of the IIF determines the ease or difficulty of transitions between
PMC, EMC, and IMC.

---

## 4. Integrative Micro-Signals (IMS)

**Integrative Micro-Signals (IMS)** are the measurable outputs of the IIF. IMS provides a
moment-to-moment snapshot of the system’s integrative dynamics. The IMS vector includes:

- modal dominance weights  
- IIF flow metrics  
- an integration index (derived from φ and cross-frequency coupling)  
- temporal coherence measures  

IMS is the empirical bridge between CCF’s geometry and observable conscious behavior.

---

## 5. Synergy Coefficient (γ)

The **synergy coefficient γ** governs the coupling between PMC and EMC. It determines
whether IMC emergence is possible. Bifurcations in γ correspond to transitions between:

- pathological oscillation  
- therapeutic breakthrough  
- post-integrative wisdom states  

γ is the critical parameter controlling the system’s ability to reorganize.

---

## 6. Dynamical System

CCF’s core dynamical system is defined by:

- logistic self-excitation  
- cross-modal inhibition  
- γ-mediated coupling  
- attractor stability analysis  

These equations formalize the transitions between collapse, breakthrough, and
reintegration. They provide the mathematical backbone of the framework.

---

## 7. Information-Geometric Manifold

CCF models consciousness within an **information-geometric manifold**. This manifold
contains:

- attractor basins  
- coherence gradients  
- stability surfaces  
- transition pathways  

The manifold provides a structured way to understand how consciousness moves, reorganizes,
and stabilizes.

---

## 8. Transformational Arc

CCF identifies a structural isomorphism between its modal transitions and the
**Hero’s Journey**. This arc is not metaphorical — it is a culturally independent
formalization of the same information-dynamic sequence encoded in CCF’s equations:

- departure → PMC destabilization  
- dissolution → EMC dominance  
- integration → IMC emergence  
- return → CCS stabilization  

This arc provides a universal template for transformation.
