## The Coherent Consciousness Framework

The CCF is structured as a **four-layer stack**, each layer describing a different scale and modality of coherence. Layers interact bidirectionally — coherence both emerges upward and is shaped downward.

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  L4 · Institutional Coherence           │
├─────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  L3 · Collective Coherence              │
├─────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  L2 · Relational Coherence              │
├─────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  L1 · Individual Coherence              │
└─────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

## Layer Descriptions

### L1 — Individual Coherence
The foundation. Coherence within a single conscious agent: the alignment of perception, cognition, affect, and action. A coherent individual maintains a stable sense-making frame while remaining adaptive to new information.

**Key constructs:** self-model stability, attentional coherence, interoceptive alignment

### L2 — Relational Coherence
How coherence propagates and negotiates between two or more agents. This layer governs shared meaning, coordinated attention, and the intersubjective field.

**Key constructs:** shared affordance space, communicative resonance, mutual modeling

### L3 — Collective Coherence
The emergence of coherent patterns across groups, teams, and communities. At this layer, distributed cognition and collective intelligence become possible — or break down.

**Key constructs:** distributed sensemaking, role coherence, knowledge commons

### L4 — Institutional Coherence
The largest scale: how organizations, institutions, and social systems encode, transmit, and renew coherence across time and turnover.

**Key constructs:** structural integrity, policy-culture alignment, generative governance

## Cross-Cutting Dimensions

Every layer is evaluated across three dimensions:

| Dimension | Description |
|---|---|
| **Signal Fidelity** | How accurately information is preserved across interfaces |
| **Adaptive Capacity** | Ability to maintain coherence under perturbation |
| **Generative Tension** | The productive tension that prevents rigidity |
