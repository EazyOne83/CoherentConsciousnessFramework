## Decision Systems

Decisions are the primary sites where coherence is either maintained or broken. The CCF provides a framework for designing decision systems that preserve coherence across scales.

## Why Decisions Break Coherence

Most organizational decision failures are not failures of analysis — they are failures of **coherence across the decision cycle**:

- The problem is framed by L4 (institution) but the information lives at L1 (individual)
- The decision is made at L3 (collective) but implemented at L2 (relational)
- The rationale is not communicated, so implementation diverges from intent

The CCF maps decision systems against these coherence breaks.

## A Coherence-Aware Decision Cycle

```
1. Signal (L1/L2): Individual or relational sensing of a condition
2. Framing (L2/L3): Collective sensemaking of the signal's significance
3. Deliberation (L3): Structured exploration of options
4. Decision (L3/L4): Commitment with documented rationale
5. Communication (L2): Translation of decision into relational context
6. Implementation (L1): Individual action aligned with decision
7. Feedback (L1→L4): Signal from implementation back to the system
```

Coherence is maintained when each step is completed with sufficient fidelity and the rationale is preserved across transitions.

## Decision Pathologies

| Pathology | CCF Diagnosis | Intervention |
|---|---|---|
| Analysis paralysis | High alignment, low adaptive capacity (rigid coherence) | Introduce time-bounded deliberation |
| Premature consensus | Low generative tension | Structured dissent practice |
| Decision reversals | L4 decision not grounded in L1/L2 signal | Richer signal collection before framing |
| Implementation drift | L2 communication failure | Decision rationale documentation + L2 briefing |
| Feedback blindness | No feedback channel from L1 → L4 | Explicit sensing rituals post-implementation |

## Design Recommendations

- **Document the rationale**, not just the decision — future coherence depends on understanding *why*
- **Map the decision to a layer** — is this an L3 or L4 call? Who needs to be in the room?
- **Protect the sensemaking step** — do not rush from signal to decision; framing is where coherence is established
- **Close the loop** — every significant decision should have a defined feedback mechanism
