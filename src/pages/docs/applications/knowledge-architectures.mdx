## Knowledge Architectures

Knowledge systems are the memory of coherence. How an institution captures, structures, and retrieves knowledge determines whether coherence can persist beyond individual tenure.

## The Knowledge Coherence Problem

Most organizations accumulate knowledge in ways that are:

- **Person-dependent** — trapped in individual minds, lost on departure
- **Format-fragmented** — scattered across email, documents, chat, and memory
- **Context-stripped** — decisions recorded without the reasoning that produced them

The result is **institutional amnesia**: the organization repeatedly encounters the same problems, makes the same mistakes, and loses the accumulated insight of its own history.

## CCF Principles for Knowledge Architecture

### 1. Preserve Rationale, Not Just Artifacts

Every decision document, policy, and procedure should carry its **reasoning layer** — the context, constraints, and tradeoffs that produced it. Without this, future actors cannot know when to follow the artifact and when to depart from it.

### 2. Structure for Sensemaking, Not Storage

Knowledge architecture is not about filing. It is about enabling **rapid sensemaking** — the ability to move from a question to a coherent understanding quickly. This means:

- Hierarchical structure (like these docs) for navigable depth
- Relational linking for cross-domain coherence
- Progressive disclosure: summary → detail → source

### 3. Design for Turnover

Assume 20-30% annual turnover in key roles. A coherent knowledge architecture should enable a new entrant to achieve working coherence within days, not months.

### 4. Close the Knowledge Loop

Knowledge systems that are write-only fail. Design explicit review cycles:

- **Quarterly**: update docs to reflect operational reality
- **Annually**: audit for policy-practice alignment
- **On major change**: trigger a structured knowledge review before implementation

## MDX as a Knowledge Architecture

This documentation site is itself a CCF-aligned knowledge architecture:

- **MDX** enables living documents — components can embed interactive diagrams, decision trees, or dynamic content alongside prose
- **Sidebar navigation** provides hierarchical sensemaking structure
- **Layer organization** (Framework → Philosophy → Applications → Reference) mirrors the CCF's own layered model
- **Open format** means the knowledge can be forked, extended, and integrated into other systems
