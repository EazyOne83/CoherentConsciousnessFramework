# Scope & Objective

The scope of CCF includes system dynamics, simulation, testability, and applied models. Its objective
is to provide a unified, operational, and testable foundation for understanding coherent systems.

This section defines the limits of the framework and clarifies what CCF aims to achieve — and what it
intentionally avoids.
