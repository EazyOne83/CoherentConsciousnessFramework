# Introduction

The **Coherent Consciousness Framework (CCF)** is a systems-informed model for understanding how coherence emerges, propagates, and sustains itself across conscious and organizational systems.

It sits at the intersection of:

- **Systems theory** and second-order cybernetics
- **Complexity science** and emergence
- **Philosophy of mind** and enactivism
- **Organizational epistemology**

## What is Coherence?

Coherence is not uniformity. It is the dynamic condition in which parts of a system maintain meaningful, adaptive relationships — where information flows without excessive noise or fragmentation, and where action aligns with intent across scales.

> "Coherence is the signature of a living system that knows itself."

## Why a Framework?

Most treatments of consciousness and coherence in organizations are either too abstract (pure philosophy) or too narrow (a single model or tool). The CCF provides a **layered, navigable architecture** that is:

1. **Grounded** in first-principles systems thinking
2. **Modular** — each layer can be studied and applied independently
3. **Extensible** — designed for your domain, institution, or research context
